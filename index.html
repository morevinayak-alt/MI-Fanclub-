Why Mumbai Indians?

Mumbai Indians isn't just a cricket team; it’s an emotion. With the famous motto 'Duniya Hila Denge,' MI has shown the world what teamwork, fight, and never-giving-up spirit look like. We have won multiple championships and created some of the biggest icons in cricket history.
