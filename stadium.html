The Fortress: Wankhede Stadium

Wankhede Stadium is the legendary home ground of Mumbai Indians. Located right next to the Arabian Sea in Mumbai, this stadium has witnessed some of the most historic moments in cricket history.

When the stadium is packed with thousands of fans dressed in blue jerseys, waving flags, and chanting "Mumbai, Mumbai!", the energy is absolutely electric. It makes it incredibly tough for any visiting team to win here.
