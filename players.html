Meet the MI Legends & Superstars

Over the years, Mumbai Indians has been home to some of the greatest cricketers in the world. Here are the key pillars of our squad:

Rohit Sharma – The Hitman

Rohit Sharma is a true Mumbai Indians legend. As our long-time captain, he led MI to five historic titles with his calm leadership and explosive opening batting. His elegant pull shots are a treat for every fan.

Jasprit Bumrah – The Yorker King

Bumrah is arguably the best fast bowler in the world. His unique bowling action and deadly accurate yorkers in the final overs make it almost impossible for batsmen to score against him.

Hardik Pandya – The Dynamic All-Rounder

Hardik Pandya provides explosive power to the team. He can smash huge sixes at the end of an innings and pick up crucial wickets with his fast-medium bowling updates.
