Unforgettable MI History & Titles

Mumbai Indians is famous for thrilling, nail-biting finishes. Here is a timeline of the most historic moments every true fan remembers:

  1. The 2017 Final – A 1-Run Miracle!
    Defending a tiny total of just 129 runs against Rising Pune Supergiant, Mitchell Johnson bowled a legendary final over to restrict them and win the trophy for MI by exactly 1 run!
  2. The 2019 Final – Malinga's Magic Slow Ball
    Once again, the tournament came down to the very last ball against Chennai Super Kings. Lasith Malinga bowled a brilliant slower ball to trap Shardul Thakur LBW, winning MI their 4th title by just 1 run!
  3. The Core Foundation
    Whether it was Sachin Tendulkar opening the batting in the early years or Kieron Pollard hitting massive sixes out of the Wankhede Stadium, MI has built a continuous layout of champions.
